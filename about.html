Our Story

Welcome to Everything Wool — a small handmade wool shop creating cosy, colourful and unique pieces.

Everything Wool is all about handmade creativity, wool and making things with care.

From cosy hats and warm scarves to useful bags, colourful purses, handmade toys and cushions, our creations are made with attention to detail and a love of crafting.

Because our products are handmade, every piece has its own character. Small differences are part of what makes handmade items special.

We are always creating and exploring new ideas, so our collection can continue to grow with new handmade wool creations.
