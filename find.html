Where to find us

You can find Everything Wool at selected local markets, craft fairs and community events. Come and see our handmade wool creations in person!

⭐ Recommended events

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

All Saints Garden Art & Craft Market

Every Saturday · 10am–4pm

A handmade-only artisan market in central Cambridge. Traders must make their own products, making this an excellent match for Everything Wool.

Where:
All Saints Garden, Trinity St,
Cambridge CB2 1TQ

Stall information:
Apply through Cambridge City Council. The PDF lists a Saturday fee of £38.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Cambourne – Christmas Market at The Hub

Saturday 28 November 2026 · 11am–4pm

A community Christmas market featuring pottery, cards, jewellery, knitted goods, food and Santa.

Where:
The Hub, High Street,
Great Cambourne CB23 6GW

Stall information:
Contact Cambourne Town Council about applications.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Cambourne Village College – South Cambs Indoor Christmas Market

2026 date not yet announced

A large indoor Christmas market. Previous events had around 100 traders, three halls and approximately 2,000 visitors.

Where:
Cambourne Village College,
Sheepfold Lane, Cambourne CB23 6FR

Previous events had free-pitch trading, so this is particularly worth asking about.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Presents Galore – Newmarket

Friday 27 – Sunday 29 November 2026

A large three-day Christmas shopping fair with more than 120 boutique stalls and a dedicated Christmas & Crafts Area for local makers.

Where:
Rowley Mile Racecourse,
Cambridge Road, Newmarket CB8 0TF

Stall information:
Contact the organisers about exhibiting.

More places to find us

Comberton Village Hall – Christmas Fayre

Saturday 5 December 2026 · 10am–12pm

A small village Christmas fayre with stalls, gifts, coffee, mince pies and Father Christmas.

Where: Green End, Comberton, Cambridge CB23 7DY

Contact: combertonvillagehall@gmail.com

Comberton Village Hall – Easter Fayre

Saturday 4 April 2026

A small community Easter event with stalls, hot-cross buns and Easter activities.

Especially suitable for wool toys and bags.

Comberton Monday Studio Art & Craft Exhibition

July 2027 likely

An arts and crafts exhibition and sale held over a whole weekend.

Please note that the hall says the event is open to people from the village.

Contact Angela Donnelly: 07731 875664 / donnella1505@gmail.com

Cambourne – Manor House Christmas Fair

Friday 27 November 2026 · 3–7pm

A smaller Christmas art and craft fair.

Where: Manor House, Cambourne CB23 6DH

Gransden Show – Great Gransden

Saturday 26 September 2026 · 9am–5:30pm

A large agricultural and countryside show with livestock, equestrian events, food, trade stands and crafts.

Where: Show Field, Caxton Road, Great Gransden, SG19 3AN

Contact: info@gransdenshow.org.uk

Little Gransden Air & Car Show

Sunday 30 August 2026

A big family day with aircraft, classic cars, entertainment and trade and craft stalls.

Trade applications are accepted through the show's trade stall booking system.

Mill Road Winter Fair – Cambridge

Saturday 5 December 2026 · 10:30am–4:30pm

Longstowe Christmas Fair

Sunday 22 November 2026
