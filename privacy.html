Privacy policy

This page explains what information may be collected when you use the Everything Wool website and how it may be used.

1. Information we may collect

If you contact us through our website, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The subject of your message
  • The contents of your message

2. Contact form

Our contact form is provided using Formspree. Information submitted through the form is sent to us so that we can respond to your enquiry.

Formspree is a separate service, so its own privacy policy may also apply to information submitted through the form.

3. How we use your information

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your messages
  • Answer questions about our products
  • Deal with enquiries or orders where applicable
  • Provide customer support

4. Sharing your information

We do not intentionally sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by services that help us operate the website, such as our contact-form provider.

5. Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Website hosting may involve technical information such as IP addresses and browser information being processed by the hosting provider.

6. Cookies

This website is designed to work without requiring unnecessary advertising or tracking cookies.

Some third-party services used by the website may have their own technical cookies or similar technologies.

7. Keeping information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect information that is provided to us. However, no method of sending information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access to or correction of information held about you.

To ask about your information, contact:

everythingwool@outlook.com

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact us

If you have a question about this privacy policy, please contact Everything Wool.

Important: This is a general privacy-policy template, not legal advice. Review it against the information your website and business actually collect before publishing it.
